Legal Resources & Guidance

1. Inheritance Laws: Under the Hindu Succession Act, daughters have equal rights to ancestral agricultural land.

2. Tenancy Rights: If you are a tenant farmer, ensure your name is registered in the revenue records (Adangal/Pahani) to claim subsidies and crop insurance.

3. Digitization of Land Records: Use your state's digital portal (e.g., Dharani in Telangana, Bhoomi in Karnataka) to verify your land titles and prevent fraud.

What is Contract Farming? It's an agreement between a farmer and a buyer for the production and supply of agricultural products under forward agreements.

Important Clauses to check:

  • Fixed pre-agreed price regardless of market fluctuations.
  • Clear quality standards to avoid unfair rejection.
  • Penalty clauses for delayed payments from the buyer.

If you face issues with land grabbing, non-payment by traders, or fake seeds/fertilizers:

  1. Mandal Revenue Office (MRO): For land disputes.
  2. District Consumer Forum: For fake seeds or defective agricultural equipment.
  3. SDM / District Magistrate: For enforcing contract farming agreements under the new acts.
