Ramesh's Transition to Organic Mangoes

After facing soil quality degradation, Ramesh shifted to 100% organic farming using Jeevamrutham. Today, his Alphonso mangoes are exported locally, fetching a 40% premium price.

Innovation Master
Telangana

Lakshmi's Drip Irrigation Success

By utilizing the PM-KUSUM scheme, Lakshmi installed solar-powered drip irrigation on her 5-acre land. She reduced water wastage by 60% and cultivated a highly profitable vegetable harvest.
