PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

â‚¹6000 per year income support to all landholding farmer families, directly deposited to bank accounts.

How to Apply
  1. Visit the official PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in).
  2. Click on the 'New Farmer Registration' block under the 'Farmers Corner' section.
  3. Enter your Aadhaar number and select your state from the drop-down menu.
  4. Fill out the registration form with your personal details, land records, and bank account details.
  5. Submit the form. You can track your application status using the 'Status of Self Registered/CSC Farmer' option.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance covering yield losses due to natural disasters, pests, or diseases.

How to Apply
  1. Go to the PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in).
  2. Click on 'Farmer Corner' to apply for crop insurance yourself.
  3. Register with your mobile number to create an account.
  4. Log in and initiate a new application by providing exact details of your land, crop sown, and bank passbook.
  5. Alternatively, you can visit your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) or bank branch where you hold an active loan.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Meet comprehensive credit requirements of the agriculture sector at very low interest rates.

How to Apply
  1. Download the KCC application form from the PM-KISAN portal or your respective bank's website.
  2. Fill out the simple one-page application form.
  3. Attach copies of land documents, Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and passport-size photographs.
  4. Submit the completed form directly at the bank branch where you have a savings account.
  5. The bank will verify your landholdings and issue a KCC limit based on your crops and acreage.

Soil Health Card Scheme

Get a detailed report on the nutrient status of your soil, along with recommendation on crop-wise fertilizer usage.

How to Apply
  1. Contact your local agriculture department official or village sarpanch.
  2. Officials will visit your farm to collect soil samples.
  3. The samples are tested at mobile or permanent soil testing laboratories.
  4. Once tested, the Soil Health Card will be generated online (soilhealth.dac.gov.in).
  5. You can download it using your mobile number or farmer ID from the portal.

Subsidies to install solar pumps and grid-connected solar power plants to ensure energy security.

How to Apply
  1. Visit the official KUSUM portal (mnre.gov.in/kusum) or your State's Renewable Energy portal.
  2. Select the applicable Component (A, B, or C) for your farm (e.g., standalone solar pump).
  3. Register online by providing your Aadhaar, bank details, and farmland Khatauni/Khasra documents.
  4. Pay the farmer's share (usually 10%) online once your application is approved.
  5. The vendor assigned by the state government will survey your field and install the solar pump.
